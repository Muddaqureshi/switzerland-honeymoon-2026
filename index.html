🏞️ Ruinaulta — the "Swiss Grand Canyon"
  • One of Switzerland's most beautiful natural landmarks: huge white cliffs · turquoise Rhine River · pine forests · panoramic viewpoints
  • Unlike the Alps, this is a giant river gorge.
  • ✅ LOCKED PLAN — walk it. 🚶 Park at Parkhaus Stenna and walk: Stenna ↓ Restaurant Conn ↓ Il Spir viewing platform. About 45–60 min each way on a flat forest trail — roughly 1.5–2 hours of walking round trip, comfortably inside your 3-hour limit.
  • 🎒 Bring water and comfortable shoes. Easy, mostly flat, well-marked.
  • 🐴 Backup (low effort): seasonal horse-drawn carriage, Flims Waldhaus → Restaurant Conn (~CHF 15–20 shared / ~CHF 80 private — book via Restaurant Conn)
  • 🚲 E-bikes only if you pre-book a specific provider. Do not assume bikes will be available on the day — "rent e-bikes nearby" is not a plan. If you do reserve one, the same route takes just 15–20 min.
  • ⚠️ The Conn forest road is closed to cars — walkers, bikes & carriage only. You cannot drive to Il Spir.
10:15 AM–12:00 PM

🅿️ Parking & Getting Around

Prices are approximate — Swiss tariffs vary, so confirm on the meter. Most lots accept Visa / Mastercard / Apple Pay.

📏 Universal rules — memorise these three
  • Barrier garages: take a ticket on entry → pay at the machine BEFORE walking back to the car → insert the paid ticket at the exit gate. (Apple Pay / Visa / Mastercard accepted.)
  • Open municipal lots: read the machine first — you may need to enter your license plate rather than display a ticket.
  • ⛽ Fuel: refuel around Flims / Chur, or just before the Zurich Airport return. Confirm the rental company's fuel policy and return the car at the required level.
  • 🔒 Car security: never leave passports, medication, electronics or visible luggage in the parked car — including at Lake Cauma, Grindelwald Terminal and Lauterbrunnen.
