🎒 Packing Essentials

  • Packable down jacket / fleece — Männlichen (2,343 m) is 10–15°C cooler than the valley, even in July.
  • Waterproof shell — afternoon alpine thunderstorms are common July–Aug.
  • Swimwear — Lake Cauma (~22°C in August).
  • Grippy walking shoes for meadow/ridge paths.
  • High-altitude sun protection (UV is stronger above 2,000 m).
  • Offline maps downloaded (signal is patchy in some valleys).
